# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The Minaa team — a small business in Kuwait running a warehouse and long-term parking reservations platform (منصة ميناء). They use this tool to track recurring and one-time business payments (software subscriptions, service bills, domain/hosting, equipment rentals) across KWD, USD, and EUR. Used on both desktop and mobile, by multiple team members sharing one login, to check what's owed, add or edit payments, and generate invoices when needed.

## Product Purpose

Give the Minaa team a single, always-current shared ledger of monthly, quarterly, and one-time payments — accessible from any device — so anyone on the team can see accurate totals, add a new payment in seconds, and produce a printable invoice without spreadsheets or guesswork.

## Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, and now playfully tactile. Friendly, human, Arabic-first in tone — not a cold corporate dashboard. The app has embraced a soft, "squishy" interactive feel (via the Jelly UI component library) as part of its personality: buttons and controls respond with a tangible, tactile bounce. The warmth still comes from color and directness — the orange seagull-on-bollard logo, plain-spoken Arabic labels — but playful, tactile interaction is now a deliberate, embraced part of the brand, not something to minimize.

## Anti-references

- Not a sterile enterprise SaaS dashboard (grey-on-grey, dense data tables, corporate jargon).
- Not garish or gamified (no confetti, badges, or forced cheerfulness).

## Design Principles

- Clarity over cleverness — totals and payment amounts must be instantly scannable at a glance.
- One blue, one red, cream paper. Minaã Blue carries all data and actions; Minaã Red is reserved for destructive actions. Payment types are told apart by icon and label rather than by hue, so colour keeps a single meaning everywhere — including the invoice.
- RTL-first, Arabic-first — layout decisions are made for right-to-left reading order natively, not adapted from an LTR default.
- Equally usable on a phone in the field and a desktop in the office — no feature or piece of information should be desktop-only.
- Friendly, not decorative — warmth comes from color and plain language, not ornamentation or gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accommodations required beyond standard web accessibility practice (readable contrast, tappable target sizes, no reliance on color alone where avoidable).
