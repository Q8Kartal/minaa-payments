Jelly UI wearing Minaã. Every control below is the real Jelly component, all but unmodified — the identity comes from one token bridge that replaces Jelly's design language with ours: eleven colour tokens, the 14-step spacing scale, and 29LT Idris Round.

Theming · 01

<jelly-theme>

The provider that makes every other entry on this page Minaã. It scopes the complete token set to its subtree, and canvas-painted components resolve those tokens at draw time — which is why the bridge is applied here rather than on :root alone. The page runs at auto, so it follows your operating system and flips live when you change it.
