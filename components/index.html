Forms · 04

<jelly-checkbox>

A form-associated checkbox with checked and mixed states. The two modes are not one rule applied twice: light draws a Secondary 600 mark on a Primary 300 box, dark a Neutral 100 mark on a Secondary 600 box. Both are drawn that way in the Figma file rather than derived from each other.

Forms · 05

<jelly-input>

A single-line field. Focus lifts and rings the membrane, and each keystroke ripples the surface at the caret. Focus shows twice over: the permanent Secondary 600 edge turns Primary 700, and Jelly paints a ring of its own on the canvas beneath it. That doubling is measured and recorded in the stylesheet rather than hidden. Neither is the 3px solid ring the Buttons page specifies; the two libraries do not agree on focus yet.

<jelly-range>

Two knobs picking a low/high interval, each carrying its own focus and ARIA. Track and thumb share the slider's geometry so the two read as one family.

Forms · 11

<jelly-segmented>

A capsule track of mutually exclusive options with a pill that slides, leans and stretches between segments. roles chooses whether it announces as a radiogroup or a tablist — the look is identical, the semantics are not.

Forms · 14

<jelly-switch>

A draggable switch whose thumb stretches like a liquid drop and whose track crossfades between off and on. The track carries the state: off is Primary 300 in light and Neutral 100 in dark, on is Primary 700 in both. The thumb stays Secondary 600 throughout.

Forms · 15

<jelly-textarea>

Multi-line text that auto-grows between a minimum and maximum height while the membrane follows. Radius-24 rather than the field capsule, because a growing box cannot stay a capsule.

Theming · 16

theme switch

A <jelly-switch> at the geometry of Figma 253:26, driving this page's <jelly-theme>. Checked is light: the drawing rests the thumb at the start for dark and at the end for light, and in an LTR page the start is the unchecked position. The icon sits in whichever half the thumb has left, and the two cross-fade rather than swap, because a swap at the end of the travel pops while the track colour is still crossfading.

Feedback · 17

toasts

The toasts from the Buttons page, unchanged and deliberately identical in both modes: a toast is a momentary overlay, and the four tones are semantic colours, which are never brand and therefore never mode-dependent. An error is Orange 500 whatever is behind it. Jelly gives every toast the same part="toast" with nothing to say which of the four it is, so the tone is read back off the dot and a second part name added; that is the only reason a per-tone colour is reachable from a stylesheet at all. Meaning never rests on the colour: each tone carries its own mark, and Jelly speaks a prefix for a screen reader.

Fires into the page’s one <jelly-toaster>, bottom centre. The buttons are page furniture rather than a specimen — <jelly-button> is not an entry in this library — so the snippet below shows the call, not them.

Overlays · 18

<jelly-dialog>

A modal panel. It is not a native <dialog> and never enters the top layer — it is fixed-position with its own backdrop — and on open it moves itself to document.body. The bridge still reaches it there, but a scoped <jelly-theme> does not: a dialog written inside one renders in the page’s mode, not the scope’s. The scrim is ours rather than Jelly’s, which derives it from the text colour and so washed the page cream in dark and red in light.
