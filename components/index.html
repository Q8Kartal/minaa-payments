Forms · 04

<jelly-checkbox>

A form-associated checkbox with checked and mixed states. The mark is Neutral 100 on the Primary 700 fill, the same pairing every brand-filled control uses.

Forms · 05

<jelly-input>

A single-line field. Focus lifts and rings the membrane, and each keystroke ripples the surface at the caret. The focus indicator is Jelly's own, painted on its canvas and tinted Primary 700 by the bridge — not the 3px solid ring the Buttons page specifies. The two libraries do not agree on this yet; the difference is recorded rather than hidden.

Forms · 06

<jelly-label>

Pairs with any control through for: clicking focuses the target and the text becomes its accessible name. Prefer this over the label attribute whenever the name should be visible.

Forms · 07

<jelly-otp>

One box per digit, with typing, backspace, arrow and paste handling. Boxes are radius-16 rather than the capsule the text field uses, so a code reads as discrete cells rather than one continuous run.

Forms · 08

<jelly-radio>

Radios sharing a name under the same root form a group with roving tabindex and direction-aware arrow keys. The dot is Neutral 100 on Primary 700.

Forms · 09

<jelly-radio-group>

A labelled container that carries the legend, the layout and the size for its radios — including ones slotted in later.

Forms · 10

<jelly-range>

Two knobs picking a low/high interval, each carrying its own focus and ARIA. Track and thumb share the slider's geometry so the two read as one family.

Forms · 11

<jelly-segmented>

A capsule track of mutually exclusive options with a pill that slides, leans and stretches between segments. roles chooses whether it announces as a radiogroup or a tablist — the look is identical, the semantics are not.

Forms · 12

<jelly-select>

A combobox whose trigger and option panel are both soft-body surfaces; the panel unfolds out of the field and flips above it when the viewport has no room below.

Forms · 13

<jelly-slider>

A single value on a track, backed by a hidden native range input so it still participates in a form and answers to the keyboard.

Forms · 14

<jelly-switch>

A draggable switch whose thumb stretches like a liquid drop and whose colour crossfades between off and on. Off is Neutral 200, on is Primary 700.

Forms · 15

<jelly-textarea>

Multi-line text that auto-grows between a minimum and maximum height while the membrane follows. Radius-24 rather than the field capsule, because a growing box cannot stay a capsule.
